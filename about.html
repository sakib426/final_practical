❤️ ABOUT MEALFINDER

Food Makes People Happy

MealFinder is a simple and creative food discovery platform designed to help people find delicious recipes from around the world.

OUR STORY

Discover food. Discover joy.

MealFinder uses TheMealDB API to provide information about different meals and recipes. Users can search for a particular meal and instantly receive useful information such as meal ID, meal name, category, image and cooking instructions.


Our goal is to make recipe discovery simple, attractive and enjoyable for everyone. Whether you are looking for pasta, chicken, seafood or something completely new, MealFinder helps you discover it.
